⚙️ How It Works

1. Upload a Leaf Image

Choose a clear tomato or potato leaf photograph.

2. AI Image Analysis

The trained MobileNetV2 model analyses the image.

3. View Predictions

See the top three predicted categories and their confidence scores.

4. Check the Warning

A low-confidence warning appears when the highest score is below 60%.

2. AI prediction

Choose or capture a leaf image to begin.
Important
